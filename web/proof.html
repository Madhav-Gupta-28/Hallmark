Check it yourself

This page computes almost nothing. It canonicalises a quote — deterministic key-sorting you can read in twenty lines — then asks hallmarkfi.near for every number. The crypto, the arithmetic and the verdicts all come from the contract that guards real money. There is no backend, so there is nothing between you and the chain, and no figure here this page could have invented.

A live quote, priced by the contract

Fetches a fresh signed quote from the public 1Click API — dry:true, so it is free and commits nothing — canonicalises it here in your browser, and sends those exact bytes to the vault.

The three signed captures in the repository. These need nothing from the venue, so they work even when 1Click is down — which it was, for forty minutes, on 2026-11-02.

Or paste your own

The canonical signed JSON and its base58 signature. Pre-filled with the honest fixture. Tamper with one character of the JSON and watch is_signed go false — the contract hashes the bytes before it parses them, so no field can be edited after the venue signed.

The same calls, from your terminal

Nothing on this page is privileged. Every number comes from view calls anybody can make, and here they are without the browser. 

# the key the contract trusts
near contract call-function as-read-only hallmarkfi.near quote_signer \
  json-args '{}' network-config mainnet now

# the guaranteed-worst cost of the 99.99% fixture
near contract call-function as-read-only hallmarkfi.near worst_cost \
  json-args "$(python3 -c 'import json;print(json.dumps({"canonical":open("contract/fixtures/worst.canonical.json").read()}))')" \
