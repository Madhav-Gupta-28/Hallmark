Hallmark

A not-held order that enforces its own execution bound.

A not-held order hands discretion over the time and price of your trade to somebody else. For a century the only thing protecting the client was a promise — the best execution obligation. NEAR Intents signs the economics of every fill, so on NEAR that promise can be a number instead.

What this is not

  • Not best execution. Hallmark proves a bound and enforces a realised cost, not optimality. With one filler, first‑come‑first‑served, the filler delivers near the floor and keeps the spread.
  • Not an independent price benchmark. Of 8,992 Rhea pools, zero hold Intents‑USDC paired with wNEAR. There is no on‑chain alternative to the venue's own signed marks.
  • Not immune to a compromised venue. If 1Click's signing key is stolen, the guarantee degrades from cryptographic to “the ceiling the owner signed”.
  • No live filler ecosystem. Three fillers exist and all were written or adapted by us.
